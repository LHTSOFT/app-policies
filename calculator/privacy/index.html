LHT Calculator — Privacy Policy

Publisher: LHTSOFT
Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how LHT Calculator ("the App") handles information. LHTSOFT provides this App and uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

1. Information you enter in the calculator

Calculations and locally stored calculator history, where enabled, are used to provide calculator functionality. LHTSOFT does not operate an account service for the App and does not intentionally transmit your calculations or calculator history to LHTSOFT servers.

2. Advertising and third-party data

The App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect or access information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, app interaction data, diagnostics, and approximate location inferred from network information, subject to applicable law, device settings, and your consent choices. This information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalization. The App does not control independent processing performed by Google or its partners.

For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance for publishers.

3. Consent and privacy choices

Where required, the App requests advertising privacy choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before making ad requests. In applicable regions, a Privacy choices entry is available in the App's menu so you can review or update your choices. You can also use Android device settings to control advertising identifiers and other privacy preferences. Your choice may affect which ads are served, but not the calculator's essential functions.

4. Storage, security and retention

App settings and calculation history may be stored on your device. You can clear calculation history in the App where available and clear the App's local storage using Android settings. Data processed independently by Google is subject to Google's retention and security practices.

5. Sharing

LHTSOFT does not sell your calculation history. Information handled by Google AdMob may be processed or shared by Google and its partners as described in their published policies and applicable consent notices.

6. International processing

Advertising providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, subject to applicable safeguards and laws.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose calculator and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child's personal information was processed inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when App functionality, advertising practices, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest published version.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, visit https://lhtsoft.com/.
